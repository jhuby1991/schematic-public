Wired Network Notes
  • RAK-LINK supports up to 4 RAK8's or 32 circuits of control.
  • RAK-LINK powers up to 30 control panels for up to 1500M of CAT5/6 cable. Extra panels or longer cable runs will require additional RAK-LINKs.
  • DIN-LINK support up to 64 DPU for the DIN modules and power up to 30 control panels.
  • Rako Wired Network should be wired loop-in/loop-out unless a RAK-STAR distribution unit is used.
  • This is only a guide on how to run cables for this system, please speak to your local salesperson for specific system information.
